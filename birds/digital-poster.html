Let the birds nest before we cut the hay

Mowing early can destroy nests, but waiting can lower the quality of the hay. This proposal asks whether people who value the birds would contribute $25 each to help compensate a willing farmer for delaying the cut.

Nothing is being collected. A vote is an opinion, not a donation or pledge.
