# Asset provenance

All fonts, geographic data and libraries are served locally with the exhibit. No map tiles, font CDN, accounts, database or tracking service is required at runtime.

- **Pretendard Variable**, Kil Hyung-jin / orioncactus. SIL Open Font License 1.1. Original file and license retained in `assets/fonts/`. Downloaded from the official repository on 2026-10-04; byte hash and URL in `data/asset-manifest.json`. https://github.com/orioncactus/pretendard
- **world-atlas 2.0.2**, land boundaries at Natural Earth 1:110m scale. Natural Earth geographic data are public domain; the world-atlas redistribution carries its ISC notice in `data/world-atlas-LICENSE.txt`. The shape data describe geographic coastlines, not agriculture. https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas
- **D3 7.9.0**, ISC license in `assets/vendor/d3-LICENSE.txt`. https://github.com/d3/d3
- **topojson-client 3.1.0**, ISC license in `assets/vendor/topojson-LICENSE.txt`. https://github.com/topojson/topojson-client
- **Texture and marks:** original procedural SVG halftone/grain and original CSS/SVG marks. This approximates risograph printing digitally with a CMYK-inspired palette and multiply compositing; it is not a scanned physical print.

The styling draws on the user's requested Seoul print-studio and neo-brutalist direction. It does not claim association with any studio or reproduce a specific designer's work.
